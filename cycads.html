Our Cycad Collection

ENCEPHALARTOS SPECIES

Preserving Living Fossils

Our nursery specializes in the cultivation of rare and spectacular South African Encephalartos species. Every cycad we grow is seed-propagated, legal, and comes with all necessary TOPS (Threatened or Protected Species) permits. From striking blue-leaf specimens to magnificent large landscape features, our collection represents decades of passion and dedication to these ancient plants.

Fascinating Cycad Facts

  • Living Fossils: Cycads have existed for over 280 million years, pre-dating the dinosaurs!
  • Slow Growers: Some large Encephalartos specimens are estimated to be over 1,000 years old.
  • Dinosaur Food: They were so abundant during the Jurassic period that it is often called the "Age of Cycads".
  • Cone Bearers: Cycads don't produce flowers. They reproduce using massive cones, some weighing up to 40kg!
