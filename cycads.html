Fascinating Cycad Facts

  • Living Fossils: Cycads have existed for over 280 million years, pre-dating the dinosaurs!
  • Slow Growers: Some large Encephalartos specimens are estimated to be over 1,000 years old.
  • Dinosaur Food: They were so abundant during the Jurassic period that it is often called the "Age of Cycads".
  • Cone Bearers: Cycads don't produce flowers. They reproduce using massive cones, some weighing up to 40kg!
