How to Find Us

From George (N2)

Take the N2 westbound from George. After approximately 10km, take the Herolds Bay turnoff. Follow Herolds Bay Road for 3km. Our nursery is on your right — look for the Garden Route Flora sign.

From Mossel Bay

Head east on the N2 towards George. Take the Herolds Bay turnoff after Groot Brak. Follow signs to Herolds Bay.
