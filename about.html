ABOUT GARDEN ROUTE FLORA

Our Story

Our Journey with Ancient Giants

Our journey began over 15 years ago out of a deep-rooted love for cycads. Nestled in the serene surroundings of Herolds Bay on the Garden Route, Garden Route Flora started as a personal collection by founders Johan and Mariaan van der Merwe. Fascinated by these living fossils, what started as a hobby blossomed into a lifelong passion.

Over the years, we meticulously grew our collection, carefully nurturing each species and adapting them to the unique coastal climate of the Western Cape. Today, our nursery stands as a testament to our dedication, offering a sanctuary where these magnificent plants can thrive and be appreciated by enthusiasts and beginners alike.

Conservation Through Cultivation

At Garden Route Flora, our mission is simple yet profound: Conservation through Cultivation. Cycads are among the most endangered plant groups in the world, facing threats from habitat loss and illegal harvesting.

We are firmly committed to propagating our cycads exclusively from seed and legally acquired parent plants—never wild-harvested. By providing legally grown, high-quality plants to the public, we help reduce the pressure on wild populations. We also strive to educate our community about cycad care and conservation, ensuring these ancient species endure for future generations to marvel at.

100% Legal & TOPS Compliant

We take our legal and ethical responsibilities seriously. As a registered dealer, every cycad purchased from Garden Route Flora comes with the necessary TOPS (Threatened or Protected Species) permits and documentation. You can buy with confidence knowing your plants are legally sourced and fully compliant with South African conservation laws.
