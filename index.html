The Fascination of Cycads

Cycads are often referred to as "living fossils" because they have remained largely unchanged for over 280 million years, surviving the age of the dinosaurs. These majestic plants command respect not only for their resilience but also for their architectural beauty.

Did you know that South Africa's national tree is a cycad? Our country is home to more than 65 diverse Encephalartos species. Due to historical over-collection and habitat loss, many of these species are now TOPS (Threatened or Protected Species) protected.

At Garden Route Flora, we believe in conservation through cultivation. By growing our cycads legally from seed, we help preserve these incredible plants while making them accessible to enthusiasts without impacting wild populations.
